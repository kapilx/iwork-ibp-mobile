## Claim Progress Tracker

- Claim progress tracker will have multiple steps and sub-steps 
- All these multiple steps and sub-steps can be configured from database 

- Claim Intimation
  - Claim Informed
  - FNOL Sent to Insurer

- Survey & Documentation
  - Loss Adjuster Appointed
  - Survey Completed
  - Documents Collected
  - Joint Inspection Report
  - LOR (Letter of Req.)

- Assessment & Validation
  - Track Document Submission
  - Assessment Report
  - Validation of Report

- Settlement
  - Claim Settlement
  - Discharge Voucher Gen.
  - Customer Agreement
  - Voucher to Insurer
  - Claim Payment


#### Lookup values  required

- Document Types master can be lookupvalue , can be configured from database 

- Document types 
  - Policy Copy
  - FIR
  - Invoices
  - Photos
  - Estimate
  - Other
