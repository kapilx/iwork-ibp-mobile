The Employee Insurance Portal is your central place to review and complete your insurance enrollment.

How to Access the Portal

 
  1. Click the portal link below.
  2. If you have not set your password yet, choose the available option such as Email or Phone on the login screen.
  3. Enter your registered email address or mobile number in the selected tab.
  4. Complete the password setup or verification process using the instructions shown on screen.
  5. Once your password is set, log in to the portal.
  6. Review the listed policies and complete your enrollment within the enrollment window.
