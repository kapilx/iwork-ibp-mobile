Requirements:

- Companies may be organized as a parent and subsidiary, and this structure is part of the company's group company concept. So a HR person may have visibility into multiple companies (of the same group company). However, there is no requirement to consolidate/aggregate statistics/policies across companies. Even an employee that has access to multiple companies can only look at the HR portal of one company at a time.
- A policy intake may have several attributes tied to each employee - e.g., Location, City, Branch, Department, Business Unit, Client Company etc. The IBP portal should provide a way to limit the visibility of a HR admin's access to the employee data of one or more values of 1 specific attribute. In this phase, we will pick Company's Risk Location attribute (of the type Policy Config Location) to optionally limit HR user's visibility to the employee data. i.e., A Company (e.g., TeamLease) has 3 Risk Locations of the type "Policy Config Location" -- Amazon, Flipkart, Other. So, we may configure in HR Portal's user management that 3 HR Portal admin users from TeamLease has visibility to employee records of 1 location each, whereas their CHRO has visibility to employee records of all locations. In addition, we may also configure an external user (e.g., joefrancis@amazon.com) to have access to employee records of Amazon location only.
- Only the IIRM's CRM for the client should be able to do user management of the HR portal for that client. Any users from the client to be designated as HR admins, and their visibiilty scope restrictions, etc. will be done by the CRM. Pleae note that the client users to be designated as HR admins may be part of the inception data and they themselves may have policies that they individually need to enrol in - so, such users should be able to switch between their "employee view" and "HR view". Also some client users may not be part of the company (external users) and may have external emails that need to be validated with OTP etc., upon invitation by the CRM. Client employee users may have to be added to the HR Portal as HR Admins even before inception and/or endorsement data that includes their information is uploaded. Which means, they are added to the HR portal as admin users before their policy enrolment data is uploaded, so when the inception/endorsement is done, the users need to be merged carefully so that the HR admin can switch between their employee view and the HR view.
