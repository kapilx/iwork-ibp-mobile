**📘 SLIDE PACK: DPDP RULES --- DETAILED EXPLANATION (Rule-wise)**

**Structured presentation to Leadership, HODs, IT, Ops, and Legal.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**🟦 SECTION 1 --- RULES EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY (13 NOV 2025)**

**Slide 1 --- Rule 1: Short Title & Commencement**

**Title:** Rule 1 --- Short Title & Commencement\
**Details:**

- Official name: **Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025**.

- Published on **13 November 2025**.

- Different rules come into force on different future dates (staggered enforcement).

- Base framework becomes effective immediately.

**Why it matters:**

- Establishes the legal force of upcoming compliance timelines.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Slide 2 --- Rule 2: Definitions**

**Title:** Rule 2 --- Definitions\
**Details:**\
Critical operational definitions:

- **Data Principal:** Individual whose data is processed.

- **Data Fiduciary:** Any entity deciding *purpose & means* of data processing.

- **Consent Manager:** Board-registered entity managing user consent.

- **Significant Data Fiduciary (SDF):** Large/high-risk entities notified by Govt.

- **Processing:** Fully or partly automated operations on personal data.

- **Personal Data Breach:** Any unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

**Why it matters:**

- Defines roles and responsibilities impacting IIRM, GHITL, and group operations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**🟦 SECTION 3 --- RULE EFFECTIVE 13 NOV 2026 (1-Year Gap)**

**Slide 8 --- Rule 4: Registration of Consent Managers**

**Title:** Rule 4 --- Consent Manager Registration (Effective 13 Nov 2026)\*\*\
**Details:**

- Consent Managers **must register** with the Board.

- Requirements include:

  - Entity incorporated in India.

  - Minimum **net worth ₹2 crore**.

  - Interoperable platform certification.

  - No conflict of interest with data fiduciaries.

  - Seven-year consent record retention.

  - Mandatory audit and grievance redressal mechanism.

**Impact on IIRM / GHITL:**

- If you plan to act as your own Consent Manager → begin work NOW.

- If not → you must integrate with a registered Consent Manager by 2027.

**🟦 SECTION 4 --- RULES EFFECTIVE 13 MAY 2027 (18-Month Gap)**

These are the **core compliance rules**.\
This section forms the bulk of your IT projects, SOPs, and governance changes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Slide 9 --- Rule 3: Notice to Data Principal**

**Title:** Rule 3 --- Privacy Notice Requirements\
**Details:**\
Every notice must clearly mention:

- Purpose of data processing.

- Category of data being collected.

- Rights available to the user.

- Contact details of Data Protection Officer (DPO).

- Consent withdrawal mechanism.

- Grievance mechanism.

**Impact:**\
→ You must update all enrollment forms, claims platforms, website, mobile apps.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Slide 10 --- Rule 5: Processing by the State**

**Title:** Rule 5 --- Processing for State Functions\
**Details:**

- When Government processes data for subsidies, benefits, services.

- Not used much by private entities, but important in regulator interactions.

**Relevance to you:**

- If you access govt health databases or Ayushman Bharat integrations → apply.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Slide 11 --- Rule 6: Security Safeguards**

**Title:** Rule 6 --- Mandatory Security Controls\
**Details:**\
Data Fiduciaries **must implement**:

- Encryption, masking, pseudonymization

- Access controls + logs

- Firewalls, DLP, IDS

- Backup & recovery measures

- Vulnerability management

- Monitoring logs for 1 year

- Annual security audit

**Impact:**\
→ You must upgrade legacy systems, enable MFA, and deploy SIEM.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Slide 12 --- Rule 7: Personal Data Breach Handling**

**Title:** Rule 7 --- Breach Notifications\
**Details:**

- Notify affected individuals **immediately**.

- Notify Data Protection Board **within 72 hours**.

- Report must include:

  - Nature of breach

  - Data types affected

  - Number of people

  - Mitigation measures

**Impact:**\
→ IT + Legal + Ops must run breach drills.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Slide 13 --- Rule 8: Erasure of Personal Data**

**Title:** Rule 8 --- Mandatory Erasure\
**Details:**

- Erase data once purpose is completed.

- Retain audit logs for 1 year.

- Notify user 48 hours before deletion.

- Must integrate erasure with retention schedules (Third Schedule).

**Impact:**\
→ You need automated data retention engines.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Slide 14 --- Rule 9: Fiduciary Contact & Grievance Redressal**

**Title:** Rule 9 --- Public Contact Info\
**Details:**

- Must publish:

  - Contact of authorised person

  - DPO (if applicable)

- Establish a **90-day grievance closure** process.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Slide 15 --- Rule 10: Processing Children's Data**

**Title:** Rule 10 --- Children's Data Processing\
**Requirements:**

- Obtain verifiable parental consent.

- No tracking, profiling, behavioural ads.

- Special rules for health data.

**Impact:**\
→ If you process dependent health data under GMC, add parental consent flows.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Slide 16 --- Rule 11: Persons with Disability**

**Title:** Rule 11 --- Guardian Consent\
**Details:**

- Obtain consent from lawful guardian.

- Verification required.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Slide 17 --- Rule 12: Significant Data Fiduciaries (SDF)**

**Title:** Rule 12 --- SDF Requirements\
If notified as SDF by govt, must conduct:

- Annual Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)

- Annual Algorithm Audit

- Annual Compliance Audit

- Appoint DPO (India-based)

- Appoint independent data auditor

**Impact:**\
→ Given your volume of health claims, you may be designated SDF.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Slide 18 --- Rule 13: Exemptions (Research, Archival, Statistics)**

**Title:** Rule 13 --- Limited Processing Exemptions\
**Details:**

- Relaxed rules for:

  - Research

  - Archival

  - Statistical analysis

- But must use anonymization/pseudonymization.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Slide 19 --- Rule 14: Data Transfer Outside India**

**Title:** Rule 14 --- Cross-Border Transfer Conditions\
**Rules:**

- Govt may whitelist or blacklist countries.

- Must comply with govt-authorized transfer terms.

- Sensitive health data handling requires special care.

**Impact:**\
→ Your Sri Lanka, Maldives, Kenya offices must not access India PII without compliance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Slide 20 --- Rule 15: Government Directions**

**Title:** Rule 15 --- Government Directions to Fiduciaries\
**Details:**

- Govt can issue:

  - Urgent directions

  - Blocking orders

  - Data preservation orders

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Slide 21 --- Rule 16: Obligations of Intermediaries**

**Title:** Rule 16 --- Intermediary Responsibilities\
**Details:**

- Intermediaries facilitating consent or data transfer must comply with:

  - Technical standards

  - Audit requirements

  - Logging & reporting
