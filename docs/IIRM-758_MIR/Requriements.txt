What is MIR?
- Monthly Information Report 

Business Process:
- This is a monthly report of policies purchased by a Client/Company which is generated by CRM in the application.
- This report will also check for the other policies needed for a company based on their industry. Example: A manufacturing compnay purchased only the GMC for their employees, but when the report is generated it will have a section "Risk assement" which will have the current policy and also other policies needed for them like CGL or Cyber Policy or Fire Policy.
- Now, CRM user who is assigned to this company will review the report and and necessary remarks for each section, summarize the whole report and submit.
- Once it's submitted, then it will be visible for CRM's manager & their higher up's for review.
- Using this report CRM have to setup a "Monthlty Meeting" with Client and discuss the key insights & actions to be taken care, and hand-over the report.
- This report should be generated before 5th of every month for the previous year.
- CRM or their manager's will have access to Current Month & 2 months prior to it means at any given time they can access only 3 months of reports.


Purpose of this report:
1. A client user, who is going to consume the data will understand what is the situation of each policy they purchased like premium paid (at the time of purchase/ current as on today/ anticipated by end of the policy), claims (TAT's, delays in processing, paid vs unpaid, other details), and plan for expiry policy.
2. For CRM user, to showcase the services provided whether they are on time or delays-reasons for that, discuss about the about to expiry policy, and finally if they don't have policies related to their industry risk then pitch in for the new opportunities.


Current Process:
- Old iWork, CRM will generate it manually and there are manual entry like expiry date, risk, no analystics based on the data, very huge report and reduant data.
- Sharing to Client is manual - Send over the email. No digital/application to see the current and historic report.
- Action items are noted as remarks. There are no actual action items tracked and their status for CRM.


Enhace Process:
- New iWork, build a very user friendly screen to generate, edit and submit the report.
- Combined the data points which are related and present it a better way.
- Use AI intelligence to read the data points generated by the system for fields, apply AI too generate better insights, and for risk analysis read the client's industry and check their current policies and provide which are missing and advantage if they purchase.
- Current design has remarks to be added by CRM for each field, however we need to group logically or functionally and then provide the summary by the CRM and their manager.
- Actions/follow-ups needs to be tracked as taks to CRM and plan for the next MIR.
